01 · About

Positioning myself within urban design.

Profile
Approach
Interests

I am an emerging urban designer interested in everyday users, overlooked spaces, and the relationship between physical form and social life.

My background is in architectural design. Through urban-scale research, I have begun to think beyond individual buildings and consider how infrastructure, public space, and long-term decisions shape daily life for many different people.

This portfolio brings together selected work from the Summer semester of the MS Architecture and Urban Design program at Columbia GSAPP, including mapping, field observation, visual storytelling, and collaborative studio projects.

FocusEveryday life and inclusive urban environments
MethodsGIS, mapping, photography, animation, narrative

Future inquiry

How can urban design identify and address problems that are not immediately visible?

I want to keep asking how urban design can better support people who are often overlooked and how we can create more inclusive cities through careful observation, long-term thinking, and design.

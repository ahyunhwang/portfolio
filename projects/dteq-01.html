DTEQ 01 · GIS ANALYSIS

Public Open Space
Gaps in NYC

This GIS-based study examines uneven access to public open space across New York City. By comparing open space distribution with pedestrian activity and transit access, it reveals how the study area remains relatively underserved despite its dense urban activity and strong connectivity.

GIS Data

A series of GIS layers was used to examine New York City through physical, environmental, demographic, mobility, and public-space conditions. By comparing and overlaying these datasets, the analysis revealed spatial relationships and gaps that are difficult to recognize through any single layer alone.
